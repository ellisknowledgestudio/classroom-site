Logic Gates 💡

Inside every computer chip are billions of tiny logic gates. Each gate takes inputs that are on (1) or off (0) and gives one output. Flip the switches to light the bulb, fill in truth tables and solve circuit puzzles. Ages 11-14.

Meet the gates

Tap the switches to turn them on (1) and off (0).

1 and 0: in logic, 1 means on or true and 0 means off or false. This is the same 1 and 0 that binary uses, because inside a chip a gate is made from tiny transistor switches.
