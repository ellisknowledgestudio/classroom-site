Binary: 1s and 0s 💡

Computers store every number, letter, picture and song using just two digits: 1 and 0. Flip the binary cards to see how it works, make numbers, crack binary codes and send a secret message. Ages 8-12.

Flip the binary cards

Each card has a number of dots. Tap a card to turn it on (1) or off (0). Add up the dots on the cards that are on to get the number. Look at the cards: each one has double the dots of the card to its right.

Binary

Why binary? Inside a computer are billions of tiny switches called transistors. A switch can only be on or off, so computers use 1 for on and 0 for off. Each 1 or 0 is called a bit, and 8 bits make a byte. With 8 bits you can make every number from 0 to 255.

Secret messages

Letters are stored as numbers too. Every letter has its own code number, and the computer keeps that number in binary. A capital A is 65, which is 01000001.
