IT & Computing

Find out how computers really work, from the parts inside the case to how information travels from your keyboard to your screen. More tools coming soon.

How Computers Work

Free interactive computer explorer: tap the parts inside a computer, follow data from keyboard to screen, sort input, output and storage gadgets and take the quiz. Ages 7-11.

Binary: 1s and 0s

Free interactive binary game: flip binary cards to count in 1s and 0s, make target numbers, read binary, crack secret messages in computer code and take the quiz. Ages 8-12.

Hexadecimal: Counting in 16s

Free interactive hex tool: see how hex, binary and denary connect, practise conversions, mix colours with real hex codes and take the quiz. Ages 11-14.

Algorithms: Program the Robot

Free coding game: program a robot through mazes step by step, find and fix bugs, use repeat loops and take the quiz. Ages 7-11.

Pixels and Images

Free interactive image tool: change resolution and colour depth, paint pixel art in binary, decode secret pictures, work out file sizes and take the quiz. Ages 9-13.

How the Internet Works

Free interactive internet tool: send a message in packets through routers, see how DNS finds a website, test password strength, spot online dangers and take the quiz. Ages 9-13.

Logic Gates

Free logic gates simulator: flip switches to light a bulb through AND, OR, NOT, XOR, NAND and NOR gates, fill in truth tables, solve circuit puzzles and take the quiz. Ages 11-14.

Secret Codes: Encryption

Free encryption tool: turn a Caesar cipher wheel, encrypt and decrypt messages, crack codes with and without the key, use frequency analysis and take the quiz. Ages 9-14.

Data Storage: Bits to Terabytes

Free data storage tool: climb the units ladder from bits to terabytes, convert units, work out how many photos, songs and films fit, compare storage devices and take the quiz. Ages 9-13.

Coding Basics: Variables, If and Loops

Free coding tool: predict what block programs do, step through them to watch variables change, learn if-else and loops, find and fix bugs and take the quiz. Ages 9-13.
