The lightest and most abundant element in the universe — it makes up most of the Sun and every other star. It burns with a flame so pale blue it's almost invisible.

Why CHNOPS? Together, these six elements make up almost all the mass of every living thing on Earth. Carbon and hydrogen form the backbone of every biological molecule; nitrogen and phosphorus build DNA and proteins; oxygen keeps it all running. Learn this order and you've learned the chemistry of life itself.
