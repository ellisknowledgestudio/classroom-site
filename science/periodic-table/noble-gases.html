Noble gases

Six elements that almost never react — tap a tube to see it light up the way it really does in a discharge lamp, and learn what makes it unique.

He Helium Glows peachy-orange in a tube
Atomic number
2
Atomic mass
4.0

Lighter than air, which is why it's used to fill balloons and airships — and why breathing it makes your voice sound squeaky (don't try this, it's not safe in large amounts).

Why don't they react? Every noble gas already has a full outer shell of electrons. Atoms bond with each other to fill up empty space in that outer shell — noble gases have no empty space to fill, so they mostly keep to themselves.
