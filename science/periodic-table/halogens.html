The most reactive element on the entire periodic table — it will even react with glass. In tiny, safe amounts it's added to toothpaste and drinking water to protect teeth.

Why so reactive? Halogens have seven electrons in their outer shell — just one short of a full set. Grabbing a single electron from another atom completes that shell, so halogens react eagerly with almost anything that will give one up.
