Science Resources

Element Explorer

Tap elements to learn fun facts. A kid-friendly interactive periodic table for ages 8-11.

States of Matter Lab

Free interactive states of matter lab: heat and cool water to watch its particles, sort solids, liquids and gases, learn melting, freezing, evaporating and condensing, take the quiz and print a worksheet. Ages 7-11.

Digestive System Explorer

Tap a realistic diagram to explore every digestive organ, follow food from mouth to anus, and learn the enzymes and villi. Ages 11-14.

Circulatory System Explorer

Tap a heart diagram to explore every chamber, valve and blood vessel, follow the blood to the lungs and round the body, and compare arteries, veins and capillaries. Ages 11-14.

Respiratory System Explorer

Tap a diagram of the lungs to explore the breathing system, follow a breath from the nose to the alveoli, and watch the ribs and diaphragm move. Ages 11-14.
