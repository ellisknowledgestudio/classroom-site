Science Resources

Element Explorer

Tap elements to learn fun facts. A kid-friendly interactive periodic table for ages 8-11.

States of Matter Lab

Free interactive states of matter lab: heat and cool water to watch its particles, sort solids, liquids and gases, learn melting, freezing, evaporating and condensing, take the quiz and print a worksheet. Ages 7-11.

Digestive System Explorer

Tap a realistic diagram to explore every digestive organ, follow food from mouth to anus, and learn the enzymes and villi. Ages 11-14.

Circulatory System Explorer

Tap a heart diagram to explore every chamber, valve and blood vessel, follow the blood to the lungs and round the body, and compare arteries, veins and capillaries. Ages 11-14.

Respiratory System Explorer

Tap a diagram of the lungs to explore the breathing system, follow a breath from the nose to the alveoli, and watch the ribs and diaphragm move. Ages 11-14.

Excretory System Explorer

Tap a diagram of the kidneys and bladder to explore the excretory system, follow the journey from blood to urine, and see how the kidneys filter the blood and balance water. Ages 11-14.

Skeleton and Muscles Explorer

Tap a diagram of the skeleton to explore twelve bones, tour the joints from the skull to the knee, and bend an arm to see how the biceps and triceps work as a pair. Ages 11-14.

Cells Explorer

Tap an animal cell and a plant cell to see what each part does, follow the steps from one cell to a whole organism, and meet six specialised cells. Ages 11-14.

Nervous System Explorer

Tap a diagram to explore the brain and nervous system, see how neurones pass signals, and test your reaction time. Ages 11-14.

Reproductive System Explorer

Tap diagrams of the female and male reproductive systems to explore every organ, follow the journey from egg to baby, and step through puberty, the menstrual cycle and pregnancy. Ages 11-14.

Senses Explorer

Tap the parts of the eye, ear, nose, tongue and skin to see what each one does, then follow light, sound, smell, taste and touch all the way to the brain. Ages 11-14.

Teeth Explorer

Tap a diagram to discover what the four types of teeth do, look inside a tooth, follow a bite of apple as you chew, and see how cavities form. Ages 7-11.
