Heating makes particles move faster (orange arrows). Cooling slows them down (blue arrows).

The water cycle: the Sun heats water in seas and lakes and it evaporates into water vapour. High in the sky the vapour cools and condenses into tiny drops that make clouds. When the drops get heavy they fall as rain, snow or hail (precipitation), and the water collects in rivers and seas, ready to go round again.
