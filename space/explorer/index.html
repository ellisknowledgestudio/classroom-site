Space Explorer

You are the spaceship. Fly from world to world, see how long each journey would really take, then dive in and open the fact file. Ages 7-14.

Fact file

About the numbers

For a moon and its own planet, Space Explorer shows the average distance between them. Planets are different: they circle the Sun at different speeds, so the gap between two planets changes all the time. For those journeys the dial shows the distance between their orbits, which is about as close as the two worlds ever get. Most of the time they are much further apart.

The journey time is that distance divided by your ship's speed, as if you flew in a straight line without stopping.

All facts come from NASA. Each fact file lists its sources. The spacecraft speeds are the fastest crewed flight (Apollo 10), Voyager 1 and the top speed of the Parker Solar Probe. Nothing can travel faster than light.

More worlds are on the way: first towards the Sun, then out to the edge of the solar system.
