Why does it happen? The Earth is always spinning on its axis, an imaginary line through the North and South Poles. It takes about 24 hours to spin round once. The half of the Earth facing the Sun has day, and the half facing away has night. The Sun is not really moving across the sky: we are spinning! The Earth spins from west to east, so the Sun seems to rise in the east and set in the west. (To keep things simple, sunrise here is at 6 am and sunset at 6 pm, like places near the equator. In the UK and USA the times change with the seasons.)
