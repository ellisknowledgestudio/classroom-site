Waxing

Why does it happen? The Moon does not make its own light. The Sun always lights up half of the Moon. As the Moon travels round the Earth, we see different amounts of that sunny half. A full trip from new Moon to new Moon takes about 29½ days. The pictures show the Moon as seen from the Northern Hemisphere (for example the UK and USA). In Australia and other southern countries the Moon looks flipped, left to right.
